## Payroll tax was significantly out of date — found via research, fixed, and made fully configurable

While reviewing `PayrollDAO`'s tax calculations, checked whether the hardcoded 2024 rates were still current. They weren't, in three ways, confirmed via multiple independent, recent sources:

1. **NHIF doesn't exist anymore.** It was replaced entirely by SHIF (Social Health Insurance Fund) on 1 October 2024 — flat 2.75% of gross, KES 300 minimum, no cap. The old graduated NHIF scale (KES 150–1,700) has been defunct for 21 months.
2. **NSSF thresholds jumped again in February 2026** ("Year 4" of the NSSF Act's phased rollout). Old code used LEL=7,000/UEL=36,000 (max contribution ~2,160). Current, verified across 10+ independent sources: LEL=9,000/UEL=108,000, max contribution KES 6,480 each side. (Briefly found a conflicting KES 72,000 figure from one source — checked further and confirmed that was simply the pre-February-2026 rate, now superseded.)
3. **The Affordable Housing Levy (1.5% of gross, in force since July 2023) was never implemented at all** — a mandatory statutory deduction missing entirely from payroll.

Personal relief (KES 2,400/month) was already correctly applied — checked before assuming otherwise.

**Genuinely unresolved, flagged rather than guessed:** whether the Housing Levy reduces PAYE's taxable income base is disputed across current secondary sources — some cite a Dec 2024 amendment making it deductible, others say a prior relief was repealed and it isn't. Implemented as NOT reducing taxable income (the more specific, more consistently-cited claim), but exposed as a togglable setting rather than silently picking one side.

## Made fully configurable, per explicit request

New `PayrollTaxConfig` service — every rate (NSSF thresholds/rate, SHIF rate/minimum, Housing Levy rate + the disputed-deductibility toggle, NITA, personal relief, and the full PAYE band table) is now stored in `settings` and editable from a new **Settings → Payroll Tax Settings** dialog, rather than hardcoded. Includes a dynamic add/remove PAYE band editor and a "Reset to Defaults" button. The reasoning: these rates have now changed three times in under two years — hardcoding them just means another code update is needed every time KRA/NSSF/SHIF revise something.

The old `nhif`/`total_nhif` columns stay present for any historical data but are no longer populated.
