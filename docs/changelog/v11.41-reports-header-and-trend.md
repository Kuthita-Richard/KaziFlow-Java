# v11.41 — Reports header made real; the trend chart fixed (R1, step B)

## Removed (they did nothing)
- The "Branch: Nairobi HQ / All Branches" box (hardcoded names, no listener). `sales`, `expenses`, `purchases` and `products` do not record a branch, so a branch filter cannot be real until the data model does (guide §11).
- The "Date Range: This Month / This Week / …" box (no listener).
- "⬇ Export PDF": it exported a **CSV** of the finance summary. Nothing in the app produces a PDF report.
- "+ Schedule Report": a button for a feature that does not exist (it only showed a "coming soon" toast).

## Added
- A **Period** strip with the shared `DateRangePicker`. Revenue, expenses and net profit follow it (`TransactionDAO.getRevenue/getExpenses(DateRange)`); the revenue card compares with the previous period (a calendar month vs the month before, otherwise the same number of days) and shows the % only when the previous period had sales — otherwise it shows the real dates instead of inventing a trend. Inventory value and the stock pie are "as of today" (the strip says so); an invalid custom range keeps the last valid cards and disables the exports.
- Two honest export buttons — "⬇ Sales CSV" and "⬇ Finance summary CSV" — that export the **selected period** directly.

## Bugs fixed in the trend chart
- **It showed the oldest six months with data, not the latest** (`ORDER BY m ASC LIMIT 6`): after six months of use the chart froze on the first half-year.
- **Revenue and expenses were two independently padded lists paired by position**, so a month with revenue but no expenses (or the reverse) put the numbers on the wrong months.
- The x axis was the numbers 1–6. It now shows real months ("Jun 26 … Nov 26"). One query (`TransactionDAO.getMonthlyTotals`) returns the last 6 calendar months, zero-filled, revenue and expenses on the same keys; refunded/pending sales are not revenue.

## Verified
`tools/checks/ReportsViewCheck` (21 assertions; mutation-tested 8/8): no fake controls; the picker present; KPI cards for this month / last month / all time / a single custom day; the comparison % (and its absence); inventory unaffected by the period; the chart's months, alignment, window (an 8-month-old sale and expense are excluded) and flat zero on an empty database; the exports pass the selected period and are disabled while it is invalid. The screen was rendered and looked at (and a squeezed "PERIOD" label fixed). `InventedFiguresCheck` updated for the new captions. Full regression: 20 suites + renderer, 0 failures; JUnit 240 tests, the same 25 pre-existing failures.

## Still raw
The "Browse Reports" cards are plain exports (R2 replaces them with real analytics). Revenue here is sales `total_amount` (VAT inclusive) and "net profit" is revenue minus recorded expenses — a cash-style view, not accounting profit (R3).
