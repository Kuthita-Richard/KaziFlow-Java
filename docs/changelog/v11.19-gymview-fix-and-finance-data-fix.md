## Second, found independently while continuing the review: FinanceView was displaying fabricated financial data

- **Where:** `src/main/java/com/kaziflow/views/FinanceView.java`
- The "Chart of Accounts" panel — presented as real balance-sheet data — was built entirely from **arbitrary percentages of revenue/expenses**, not any actual underlying figures:
  - Cash & Bank = revenue × 0.45
  - Accounts Receivable = revenue × 0.12
  - Inventory Value = revenue × 0.97
  - Accounts Payable = expenses × 0.19
  - None of these percentages were derived from anything — they were just made-up multipliers dressed up as a real chart of accounts.
- **VAT Liability was double-counted, in two separate places**, including a dedicated **VAT Summary report page** a business would plausibly rely on for actual KRA filing:
  - `getTotalRevenue()` sums `total_amount`, which already includes VAT (`subtotal + vat_amount`). Multiplying that by 16% again computes VAT-on-VAT-inclusive-revenue — a real overstatement of liability.
  - The VAT Summary page's "Input VAT" was computed as 16% of general **expenses** (rent, utilities, etc.) — the wrong basis entirely. Input VAT should come from actual **purchases**, which already track their own real `vat_amount` per transaction.
- A "+23% vs last month" revenue comparison was also a hardcoded placeholder, not computed from anything.

## Fixed with real data throughout

- New `TransactionDAO` methods: `getTotalOutputVAT()` (real sum of `sales.vat_amount`), `getTotalInputVAT()` (real sum of `purchases.vat_amount`), `getNetVATPayable()` (output − input, floored at zero — a net input-VAT credit doesn't become a negative liability).
- Chart of Accounts now pulls from real sources: Accounts Receivable from `DeniDAO.getTotalOutstanding()` (actual Deni Book debt), Inventory Value from `ProductDAO.getTotalStockValue()` (actual stock valuation), Accounts Payable from `PurchaseDAO.getTotalOutstanding()` (actual unpaid supplier balances), VAT Payable from the new net-VAT calculation.
- **One line kept as an honest approximation, clearly labeled as such**: "Cash & Bank" is now net cash flow (revenue − expenses), since this app has no dedicated running bank-account ledger separate from revenue/expense totals — labeled "(est. from net cash flow)" in the UI rather than presented as a precise figure.
- Revenue comparison now uses `SaleDAO.getMonthRevenue()` for a real this-month-vs-last-month percentage, with a sensible message when there's no prior-month data to compare against yet.
